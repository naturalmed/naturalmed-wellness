The website is bilingual. English articles and Portuguese articles are managed separately in the CMS, build into separate folders, and have their own listing pages. The newsletter is generated only for English articles.

🗺️

  • Articles (EN) → saved to _content/articles/ → published at en/articles/YYYY-MM-slug.html → listed at en/articles.html
  • Artigos (PT) → saved to _content/articles-pt/ → published at pt/artigos/YYYY-MM-slug.html → listed at pt/artigos.html

English article fields guide

FieldWhat to write
Title required The full title in English, e.g. "Acupuncture and Chronic Pain: A TCM Perspective"
Slug required A short URL-friendly version of the title using only lowercase letters, numbers and hyphens. E.g. acupuncture-and-chronic-pain. No spaces, no accents, no punctuation.
Date required The publication date. Click the calendar icon and select today's date, or the date you want the article to appear.
Category required Select one from the dropdown: Acupuncture, Herbal Medicine, Chinese Dietetics, Qi Theory, Tuina Massage, Women's Health, Mental Wellbeing, or Seasonal Health.
Reading time Estimated minutes to read. A rough guide: count the paragraphs and divide by 3. Default is 8 minutes.
Excerpt required 2–3 sentences summarising the article in English. This appears in the newsletter email and on the articles listing page. Maximum 250 characters.
Cover image Optional. Click to upload a photo (JPG or PNG, ideally 1200×630 pixels). The same image can be used for both the EN and PT versions of the same article.
Body required The full article text in English. Use the toolbar buttons for headings, bold, italic, bullet points and links.
✍️
Writing tips for the Body field
  • Use ## Heading for section titles (click the H2 button in the toolbar)
  • Use **word** for bold text (or click the B button)
  • Use - item for bullet points (or click the list button)
  • Leave a blank line between paragraphs

The Newsletter

Every time you publish a new article, a newsletter is automatically generated and published at a private URL. You then send it to your subscribers directly from Gmail.

📨
Newsletter URL pattern

Each newsletter lives at:
https://www.naturalmed-wellness.com/newsletter/YYYY-MM-slug.html

Example: https://www.naturalmed-wellness.com/newsletter/2026-04-acupuncture-and-chronic-pain.html

The slug in the URL is the same as the one you entered in the article form. The year and month are added automatically from the publication date.

Managing Subscriber Contacts

There are two ways someone joins the mailing list, and both are automatic in Clinic Manager:

  • Website sign-up form — visitors subscribe directly on the Articles page. This is captured instantly in Clinic Manager, no manual step needed.
  • Existing patients — every active patient with an email address is included automatically, unless they have asked to be opted out.
📇
Google Contacts is a separate list that can drift out of sync

The "Newsletter NaturalMed" group in Google Contacts is what you actually send to (via Bcc), but the master list of who should be there lives in Clinic Manager. The two can drift apart over time — a patient's email changes, someone new signs up, someone asks to be removed. The check below compares the two and tells you exactly what to fix, without ever wiping the Gmail group.

👥
Who does what

The administrator (the developer, who has access to Django Admin and to the server) checks the Gmail group, fixes it, sends the newsletter and marks bounced emails. Clinic staff without Admin access work in the normal Clinic Manager screens: they correct patient records, ask patients for the right email, use the "Opt Out of Newsletter" button on a record, and decide about people who are not in the system. Everything marked "(administrator)" below is not available to clinic staff.

Before sending each newsletter: verify the Gmail group (administrator)

Since October 2026, this is a one-click check from Clinic Manager instead of a manual export/clear/re-import — and it never deletes anything from Google Contacts on its own, it only tells you what to change.

1
Open Newsletter Subscribers in the Admin

Go to clinic.naturalmed-wellness.com/admin/, log in, and under NEWSLETTER click Newsletter Subscribers.

2
Click "Verificar Newsletter (CSV)"

This button sits next to "Add Newsletter Subscriber", above the list. It compares Clinic Manager's eligible recipients against the real members of the "Newsletter NaturalMed" group in Google Contacts, live, and downloads a CSV report straight to your ~/Downloads folder — no Google Contacts export needed on your side.

3
Open the CSV and follow each row's instruction

Every row has an action in the first column and a "Nota" column that explains the reason and your options. The rows that matter most come first:

REMOVER DO GRUPO — this person asked to leave (they used the unsubscribe link in a newsletter, or opted out with you) or their email bounced (the address does not exist), but is still in the Gmail group, so they would still be sent the newsletter. In contacts.google.com, open the Newsletter NaturalMed group and take this contact out of it (you do not need to delete the contact). Do this before sending. Never register people who asked to leave again in Newsletter Subscribers. If the reason is a bounced email, the Nota says so: ask the patient for the right address (see "After sending each newsletter" below).

CORRIGIR EMAIL — the email stored on the patient's record is not a valid address (for example just a first name), or it bounced after an earlier newsletter and is already out of the group. Confirm the right address with the patient and correct it on their record in Clinic Manager. The bounce marker clears itself when you change the email; run the check again and they will show up as "ADICIONAR AO GRUPO".

ADICIONAR AO GRUPO — this person should be receiving the newsletter but isn't in the Gmail group yet. Go to contacts.google.com, open the Newsletter NaturalMed group, add a new contact with this email, and apply the group label to it.

REVER NO GRUPO — this email is in the Gmail group but has no active record in Clinic Manager: either there is no record at all, or it belongs to an inactive patient. This is not an opt-out. It may be a legitimate subscriber from before this system existed, so decide case by case. To keep them, go to Admin → Newsletter → Newsletter Subscribers → Add, enter the email and type legacy in the Source field (or reactivate the patient record); they then stop appearing in the report. To drop them, take them out of the Gmail group.

⌨️
Terminal alternative (for the developer)

The same check can be run from the server, which prints the identical comparison straight to the terminal instead of a CSV:

docker exec naturalmed-web-1 python manage.py check_newsletter_consent --group "Newsletter NaturalMed"

🔑
If the button shows an error instead of downloading a file

A red message at the top of the page (for example "Nao foi possivel verificar o grupo do Gmail") means Clinic Manager could not reach Google Contacts — almost always because the Google authorisation needs renewing. There is nothing to fix on your side: tell your developer, who can renew it in about ten minutes (see "For the developer" below). Until it is renewed you can still send the newsletter to the existing group; the check only helps keep that group up to date.

After sending each newsletter: bounced emails

Any mailing list has a few wrong addresses: a typo made years ago, or an account that no longer exists. Shortly after you send, the Gmail inbox of naturalmed.wellness@gmail.com receives automatic messages from "Mail Delivery Subsystem" for each address that failed.

1
Tell the two kinds of message apart

"Address not found" is permanent: the address does not exist or has a typo. These are the ones to act on. "Inbox full" or "temporary problem" is temporary: ignore it, the address may be perfectly fine.

2
The administrator marks them

In Gmail, open the newsletter's conversation, press Expand all, and copy the text (or just note the "Address not found" addresses). The administrator marks those patients in Clinic Manager with one command, which takes about a minute (see "For the developer" below).

3
Clinic staff fix the addresses as patients answer

After the marking, those patients appear in Reports → Patient Email Problems, with a warning on their record (see the next subsection). Ask each patient for the correct email and change it on their record: the marker clears itself and they become recipients again. In the meantime the administrator's next group check lists them under REMOVER DO GRUPO while the address is still in the Gmail group (to be taken out), and under CORRIGIR EMAIL afterwards.

📮
Why not just delete them from Google Contacts?

Because Clinic Manager would still treat the address as a valid recipient and ask you, every month, to add it back. The marker is how it learns the address is dead, and it goes away on its own as soon as the email on the record is changed. You can see which address bounced, and when, on the patient's page (yellow warning at the top), in Reports → Patient Email Problems, and in Django Admin (patient record, section "Administrative").

For clinic staff: patients with email problems

An email problem means the address on a patient's record either bounced (it does not exist) or is not a valid address at all (for example just a first name). You do not need Django Admin to see or fix these. There are three places to look:

1
On the patient page

A yellow warning at the top says what happened (for a bounce: the date and the address that does not exist), and a red tag "Email bounced" or "Invalid email" sits next to the action buttons.

2
In Reports

Open Reports and click Open list on the card Patient Email Problems. The list shows active patients with the name (a link to the record), the email on record, the problem, the bounce date and the phone number. Download CSV saves the same list.

3
In the Patient Explorer

Add the filter Email Problem (bounced or invalid) and choose "yes". A saved Smart List with that filter can be reopened at any time and recalculates itself live. The Explorer can also include inactive patients; the Reports list shows only active ones.

☎️
What to do

Phone the patient (the number is in the list), ask for the correct email and confirm it letter by letter. Open the record, click Edit and change the email. The flag disappears by itself, and the patient is a newsletter recipient again from the administrator's next group check. If a patient asks to stop receiving the newsletter instead, use the "Opt Out of Newsletter" button on their record.

For the developer: how the check works and how to renew the Google authorisation

The check is read-only: Clinic Manager only has permission to read Google Contacts, so it can never change the Gmail group. The code lives in the naturalmed/clinicmanager repository: apps/newsletter/services.py (eligibility rule and Google calls), apps/newsletter/admin.py (the button) and the check_newsletter_consent management command (terminal version).

The Google credentials are two files, token.json and client_secret.json, kept on the server in /opt/naturalmed/protected_media/newsletter_sync/ (seen as /app/protected_media/newsletter_sync/ inside the container). They are deliberately not in Git. The Google Cloud project is naturalmed-auto-consultations, and the app "NaturalMed Clinic Manager" was set to In production on 5 October 2026, without verification (do not submit it for review). While an external app stays in "Testing", Google expires its tokens after 7 days; in production a token lasts until it is revoked or goes about six months unused, so the monthly check keeps it alive. The authorisation script authorize.py and the client_secret_*.json file live only in ~/naturalmed-newsletter-auth on the developer's Mac.

If the check fails with an authorisation error (for example invalid_grant), renew the token:

1
Generate a new token on the Mac

cd ~/naturalmed-newsletter-auth && source venv/bin/activate && rm -f token.json && python3 authorize.py

Sign in as naturalmed.wellness@gmail.com. On "Google hasn't verified this app" choose Advanced → Go to NaturalMed Clinic Manager (unsafe), then allow access to contacts.

2
Copy it to the server

scp ~/naturalmed-newsletter-auth/token.json root@159.69.220.0:/opt/naturalmed/protected_media/newsletter_sync/token.json

3
Fix permissions and test

On the server run chmod 600 /opt/naturalmed/protected_media/newsletter_sync/token.json, then the terminal check shown above. No container restart is needed, because the folder is a mounted volume.

Marking bounces. Paste the bounce messages copied from Gmail into a text file and run, on the server: docker exec -i naturalmed-web-1 python manage.py mark_bounced_emails --from-text - < bounces.txt. It only picks up the permanent "Address not found" messages and, by default, only simulates: add --apply to write. Addresses can also be typed in directly, and --clear removes a marker. The marker exists only for patients (a website sign-up that bounces has none yet). The rule "bounced or invalid" is defined in one place, apps/patients/email_health.py, and drives the group check, the warning on the patient page, the Reports list and the Explorer filter. Fields: email_bounced_at and email_bounced_address on Patient, migration 0017.

The Clinic Manager chat assistant also knows this procedure (its instructions are the "PHASE 9" section of the system prompt, edited under Admin → Chat Assistant → Chat Configurations; a copy is kept in the repository as docs/CHAT_SYSTEM_PROMPT.txt).

If a patient asks to opt out

Start in Clinic Manager: open the patient's record and click "Opt Out of Newsletter". They are then excluded from the eligible list automatically. They are not removed from the Gmail group automatically, though — the next time you run the newsletter check they will appear under "REMOVER DO GRUPO", and you then remove them from the "Newsletter NaturalMed" group in Google Contacts. Until you do, they would still receive the newsletter.

If a website subscriber (not a patient) asks to opt out

They can unsubscribe themselves at any time using the link included at the bottom of the newsletter email — always keep this link in the email you send. If they contact you directly instead, ask your developer to remove them from Clinic Manager (requests that arrive by email are covered in the next section). Either way, once they have left they appear under "REMOVER DO GRUPO" the next time you run the newsletter check, and you then take them out of the Gmail group.

If someone asks to leave by email (a reply to the newsletter, or an "Unsubscribe" message)

The unsubscribe link at the bottom of the newsletter is the only way Clinic Manager finds out by itself. Someone who simply replies ("stop emailing me", "unsubscribe", "please remove me") writes to the clinic's Gmail inbox, and nothing in Clinic Manager sees that message. If nobody reads it and acts on it, the person keeps receiving the newsletter. Several requests sent this way between May and July 2026 went unnoticed, so there are now two safety nets:

  • Gmail label "Pedido de saida", with a star. A filter in the clinic's Gmail puts this label and a star on replies to the newsletter and on messages with "unsubscribe" in the subject. After each newsletter, open the label and read every starred conversation (in a long conversation, read the latest message).
  • Daily automatic check. Every morning (about 7am) a small script in the clinic's Google account looks for new requests and, only when it finds one, emails the administrator and the clinic inbox with the subject "[NaturalMed] N possiveis pedidos de saida da newsletter", with a link to the conversation. Every Monday it also sends "[NaturalMed] Verificacao semanal: sem pedidos de saida novos" if there is nothing new. If the Monday message does not arrive, tell the developer: the check has stopped.

When a request is real, do these three things the same day:

  • 1. In Clinic Manager, search for the person by email. If there is an active record, click "Opt Out of Newsletter" once (it is a switch: clicking twice undoes it).
  • 2. In Google Contacts (the clinic's account), remove the contact from the "Newsletter NaturalMed" label. Always do this, even if there is no record in Clinic Manager: the check cannot see people who exist only in the Gmail group.
  • 3. In Gmail, remove the star from the conversation, to mark it as dealt with.

Two things to know. The automatic check looks for typical wording (in English and Portuguese): it can miss a request written in other words, and it can flag messages that are not requests (an automatic reply, or a thank-you that quotes the footer), so always read the message before acting. And "Opt Out of Newsletter" only stops the newsletter: if the person also does not want appointment reminders or invoices by email, that is a decision for the practitioner (the email on the record would have to be removed).

🛠️
For the developer: where the daily check lives

An Apps Script project called "Pedidos de saida (permanente)" in the clinic's Google account (source: pedidos_saida_permanente.gs). It reads Gmail (read-only), sends email only to the two addresses fixed in CONFIG.AVISAR, and runs from a daily time trigger at about 7am. Permissions it holds: view email, send email as the clinic, run when not present. To run it by hand: open the project, choose verificarDiariamente and click Run. The list of requests already reported is kept in the script properties. The Gmail filter "Pedido de saida" is a second, independent net.

⚠️
Add new subscribers in Clinic Manager (or the website form) first, not only in Google Contacts

If someone wants to join and cannot use the website form themselves, ask your developer to add them in Clinic Manager rather than only in Google Contacts. That way they are part of the real, tracked recipient list — not just a name sitting in the Gmail group with no record of why they're there. (Unlike the old process, the monthly check no longer wipes the group, so a contact added only in Gmail will simply keep showing up under "REVER NO GRUPO" every month instead of being lost — but it's still better recorded properly in Clinic Manager.)

Sending the Newsletter

Once your article is published and the website has rebuilt (~1 minute), follow these steps to send the newsletter to all your subscribers.

✅
Before you start: verify the Gmail group

Run the "Verificar Newsletter (CSV)" check described in section 7 and fix any differences first, so that the right people receive this month's newsletter.

1
The newsletter email is prepared separately, not on the website

Each month, an HTML email file is prepared (based on the reference template in tools/newsletter-template/reference-example.html in the repository) with that month's full article, correctly formatted for email, and the working unsubscribe link already included. Open that HTML file in your browser to start.

2
Click "✓ Hide this bar — ready to copy"

This removes the yellow instruction bar so it does not appear in the email.

3
Select all and copy

Press ⌘ Cmd + A to select everything, then ⌘ Cmd + C to copy.

4
Open Gmail and compose a new email

Go to https://mail.google.com and click "Compose".

5
Fill in the "To:" field with your own email

Type naturalmed.wellness@gmail.com in the To field.

6
Click "Bcc" and add the subscriber group

Click the "Bcc" link (top right of the To field). Type Newsletter NaturalMed and select the group when it appears. Using Bcc means subscribers cannot see each other's email addresses.

7
Fill in the Subject line

Use: New article from NaturalMed: [Article Title]

8
Click in the email body and paste

Press ⌘ Cmd + V. The newsletter appears formatted with all colours and images.

9
Click "Send"

All subscribers receive a beautifully formatted newsletter without seeing each other's addresses.

⚠️
Always use Bcc, never To

If you add the group to the "To:" or "Cc:" fields, every subscriber will see the email addresses of all the others. This is a privacy issue. Always add the Newsletter NaturalMed group to Bcc.

Frequently Asked Questions

I saved an article but it is not appearing on the website yet.

Wait 1–2 minutes and refresh the page. The website rebuilds automatically after every save. You can check the progress at https://github.com/naturalmed/naturalmed-wellness/actions — a green tick means it completed successfully.

I want to edit an article I already published.

In the CMS, click "📰 Articles (EN)" for English articles or "📰 Artigos (PT)" for Portuguese articles. Click the article you want to edit, make your changes, and click "Save". The website updates automatically in ~1 minute.

What is the Slug field and how do I fill it in?

The slug is the URL address for your article. It must use only lowercase letters, numbers and hyphens — no spaces, no accents, no punctuation. For example, an article called "Acupuncture & Chronic Pain" would have the slug acupuncture-and-chronic-pain.

How do I find the newsletter URL for an article?

The pattern is always: https://www.naturalmed-wellness.com/newsletter/YYYY-MM-slug.html where YYYY-MM is the year and month of the article and slug is what you entered in the Slug field. Note: newsletters are only generated for English (EN) articles. Portuguese articles do not generate a newsletter.

I published a Portuguese article but it is not appearing on pt/artigos.html.

Wait 1–2 minutes and refresh the page. The build runs automatically after every save. If it still does not appear, check the GitHub Actions log at github.com/naturalmed/naturalmed-wellness/actions. Also confirm the article has a Title and a Slug — articles without these fields are skipped.

Can I publish the same article in both English and Portuguese?

Yes. Write the English version in "📰 Articles (EN)" and the Portuguese version separately in "📰 Artigos (PT)". They are independent — use the same slug and the same cover image if you wish. The EN version will generate a newsletter; the PT version will not.

A subscriber asked to be removed from the newsletter. What do I do?

If they are a patient, open their record in Clinic Manager and click "Opt Out of Newsletter" — they will be excluded automatically from the eligible list, and will show up under "REMOVER DO GRUPO" the next time you run the newsletter check, as a reminder to take them out of the Gmail group too. If they signed up through the website (not a patient), they can use the unsubscribe link at the bottom of the newsletter email, or ask your developer to remove them. If they asked by email, follow "If someone asks to leave by email" in section 7.

The Newsletter Subscribers page lists only a handful of people, but the Gmail group has over 150. Is something wrong?

No. That page lists only people who signed up through the website form. Patients are included in the newsletter automatically from their records and do not appear there. The "Verificar Newsletter (CSV)" button compares everyone — patients and website sign-ups together — with the Gmail group.

I clicked "Verificar Newsletter (CSV)" and got an error message, with no file.

Clinic Manager could not reach Google Contacts, usually because the Google authorisation needs renewing. Tell your developer (see section 7). In the meantime you can still send the newsletter to the existing group.

Can the assistant inside Clinic Manager explain the newsletter check?

Yes. Ask it, for example, "How do I check that the Gmail newsletter group is up to date?" or "How do I add someone to the Gmail group?". It gives the same steps as section 7.

A patient says they never receive the newsletter. Why?

Open their record. If the email bounced or is not valid, a yellow warning at the top and a red tag next to the buttons say so (the same patients are listed in Reports → Patient Email Problems). The other usual reasons are: they opted out, the record is inactive, or the address was never added to the Gmail group. The administrator's newsletter check lists most of these cases.
